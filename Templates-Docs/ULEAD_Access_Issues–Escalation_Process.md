# ULEAD Access Issues – Escalation Process

I spoke with Health IT (Nic Holloway, Clifford Kravit, and Ibraheem Ali) regarding the ongoing ULEAD access issues. They have opened an escalation with OHIA, so moving forward we should route new issues through the process below to help keep visibility high and prioritize resolution.

## When a PI or Research Team Reports a ULEAD Issue

If you or one of your PIs experiences a ULEAD issue:

1. Send an email to **uhitcustomersupport@mednet.ucla.edu**
2. Use the subject line:

   ```
   Ticket Needed - ULEAD Access
   ```

3. In the body of the email, provide a brief description of the issue. For example:

   ```
   Please open a ticket and forward to the ULEAD team.

   Unable to access ULEAD even after closing the browser completely and clearing the cache.
   ```

   Include any additional details relevant to the specific issue being reported.

4. CC the following contacts:

   - **Ibraheem Ali** (Health IT) – `iali@mednet.ucla.edu`
   - **Clifford Kravit** (Health IT) – `ckravit@mednet.ucla.edu`

> **Note:** Sending the email to the HIT Customer Support address will automatically generate a ServiceNow ticket.

## Why This Matters

The escalation with OHIA is intended to increase visibility into the scope and impact of these issues. Consistently documenting and escalating problems through the official support process helps:

- Demonstrate the frequency and impact of ULEAD access issues
- Support prioritization of fixes and troubleshooting efforts
- Justify additional resources being allocated to resolution
- Provide a documented record of affected researchers and projects

## Team Expectations

Please follow this process for all new ULEAD-related access issues reported by investigators or study teams. The more consistently we route issues through the official support channel, the more effective the escalation effort will be.

Thank you for helping ensure these issues are properly documented and escalated. If you encounter a situation that does not fit this workflow, let me know and I'll help determine the best path forward.
